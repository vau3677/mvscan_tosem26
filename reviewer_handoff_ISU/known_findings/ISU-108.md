## Published report facts

- **Root cause:** Lenders can replace the Oracle after loan approval.
- **Documented consequence:** After the loan is issued, the attacker uses the updateLoanParams() function to maliciously replace the oracle.
- **Documented fix:** Add validation for params.oracle == cur.oracle in the checks.
- **Report:** https://code4rena.com/reports/2022-04-abranft#h-03-critical-oracle-manipulation-risk-by-lender
