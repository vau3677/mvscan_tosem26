## Published report facts

- **Root cause:** lastFee is not updated in the handleFees function when totalSupply is zero.
- **Documented consequence:** When the basket is replenished, the cost will be calculated based on the incorrect time difference.
- **Documented fix:** Update lastFee even when totalSupply is zero.
- **Report:** https://code4rena.com/reports/2021-12-defiprotocol#h-01-wrong-fee-calculation-after-totalsupply-was-0
