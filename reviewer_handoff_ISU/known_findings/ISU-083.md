## Published report facts

- **Root cause:** During generation switching, the current generation's counter genMintCount is reset, failing to accurately record the true minting quantity of the previous generation when the next generation begins.
- **Documented consequence:** The number of new generations of entities was calculated incorrectly and allowed infinite generations of entities to be created.
- **Documented fix:** Remove the operation that resets genMintCount.
- **Report:** https://code4rena.com/reports/2024-07-traitforge#h-04-number-of-entities-in-generation-can-surpass-the-10k-number
