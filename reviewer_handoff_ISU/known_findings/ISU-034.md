## Published report facts

- **Root cause:** The tokenPrecisionMultipliers used in price calculations is not computed in real-time.
- **Documented consequence:** The attacker use old tokenPrecision Multipliers for transactions before the target price takes effect.
- **Documented fix:** Change tokenPrecisionMultipliers to real-time calculation.
- **Report:** https://code4rena.com/reports/2021-11-bootfinance#h-03-swaputilssol-wrong-implementation
