## Published report facts

- **Root cause:** pointsSum.slope is not updated when nominees are removed.
- **Documented consequence:** The slope for the removed nominees is still included in the calculation, resulting in an inaccurate calculation of the voting weight.
- **Documented fix:** Update pointsSum.slope in removeNominee().
- **Report:** https://code4rena.com/reports/2024-05-olas#h-01-pointssumslope-not-updated-after-nominee-removal-and-votes-revocation
