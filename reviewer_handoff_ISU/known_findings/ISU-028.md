## Published report facts

- **Root cause:** A variable for tracking the current period number is missing.
- **Documented consequence:** max_buy was miscalculated, causing users to incorrectly buy the next period's worth of bonds at a lower price than expected.
- **Documented fix:** Add a variable "currentEpoch" to track the current cycle number.
- **Report:** https://code4rena.com/reports/2023-03-mute#h-01-bond-max-buyer-might-end-up-buying-the-max-buy-of-the-next-epoch
