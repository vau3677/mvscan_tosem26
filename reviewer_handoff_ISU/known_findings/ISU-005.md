## Published report facts

- **Root cause:** The actionLockedBalances[payer] is not updated for the payer when transferring stakes.
- **Documented consequence:** stakedAndActionLockedBalanceOf() calculates wrong balance
- **Documented fix:** Call stakerVault.increaseActionLockedBalance to update after transferring funds.
- **Report:** https://code4rena.com/reports/2022-04-backd#h-02-function-lockfunds-in-topupactionlibrary-can-cause-serious-fund-lose-fee-and-capped-bypass-its-not-calling-stakervaultincreaseactionlockedbalance-when-transfers-stakes
