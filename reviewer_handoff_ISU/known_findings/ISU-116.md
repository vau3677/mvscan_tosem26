## Published report facts

- **Root cause:** The keroseneVault is not authorized to the KerosineManager.
- **Documented consequence:** The attacker registers the same asset to both management systems at the same time, causing the asset to be counted twice.
- **Documented fix:** Manage keroseneVault and regular vault in separate mappings, used for price calculation and authorization management respectively.
- **Report:** https://code4rena.com/reports/2024-04-dyad#h-01-design-flaw-and-mismanagement-in-vault-licensing-leads-to-double-counting-in-collateral-ratios-and-positions-collateralized-entirely-with-kerosine
