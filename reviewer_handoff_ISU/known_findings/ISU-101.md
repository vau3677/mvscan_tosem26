## Published report facts

- **Root cause:** When updating the voting proxy, both the old and new checkpoints were updated, causing the previous checkpoint to erroneously include the latest changes.
- **Documented consequence:** Incorrect vote counting affects the integrity of the voting system.
- **Documented fix:** Modify the "storage checkpoint" to "memory checkpoint" in both the delegate() and removeDelegation() functions.
- **Report:** https://code4rena.com/reports/2022-07-golom#h-10-upon-changing-of-delegate-votedelegation-updates-both-the-previous-and-the-current-checkpoint
