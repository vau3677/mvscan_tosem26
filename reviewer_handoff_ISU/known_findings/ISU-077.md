## Published report facts

- **Root cause:** Child node states are not considered when unwrapping the parent node.
- **Documented consequence:** When unblocking child nodes, the original expiration time management mechanism was disrupted.
- **Documented fix:** Add proper constraints when setting child node expiration times and destroy the parent node's CANNOT_UNWRAP permission.
- **Report:** https://code4rena.com/reports/2022-07-ens#h-02-the-expiry-of-the-parent-node-can-be-smaller-than-the-one-of-a-child-node-violating-the-guarantee-policy
