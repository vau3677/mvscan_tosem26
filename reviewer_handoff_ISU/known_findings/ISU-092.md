## Published report facts

- **Root cause:** The spend_limit is not decremented based on spent amounts.
- **Documented consequence:** config.spend_limit was not reduced after each spend, causing all subsequent spends to bypass the limit and the attacker could call the spend function multiple times.
- **Documented fix:** After the spending operation is successful, reduce the value of config.spend_limit accordingly.
- **Report:** https://code4rena.com/reports/2022-02-anchor#h-01-spend-limit-on-owner-can-be-bypassed
