## Published report facts

- **Root cause:** totalWethDelegated is not reduced when users withdraw WETH.
- **Documented consequence:** Reducing WETH reserves incorrectly resulted in a rollback due to insufficient reserves when attempting to restore the price.
- **Documented fix:** Update totalWethDelegated in the withdraw function.
- **Report:** https://code4rena.com/reports/2023-08-dopex#h-08-the-peg-stability-module-can-be-compromised-by-forcing-lowerdepeg-to-revert
