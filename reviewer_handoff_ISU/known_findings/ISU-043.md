## Published report facts

- **Root cause:** The time lock mechanism did not take effect as expected, allowing users to bypass the time lock and withdraw funds directly.
- **Documented consequence:** The withdrawWithTimelockFrom() function sets incorrect _unlockTimestamps.
- **Documented fix:** The withdrawWithTimelockFrom() function should increment the unlock timestamp by the specified duration.
- **Report:** https://code4rena.com/reports/2021-06-pooltogether#h-04-withdraw-timelock-can-be-circumvented
