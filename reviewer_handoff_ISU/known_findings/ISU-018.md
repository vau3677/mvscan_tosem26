## Published report facts

- **Root cause:** The _setKeyManagerOf function does not clear single-token approvals.
- **Documented consequence:** The attacker repeatedly transfers the key back to themselves from the victim without clearing the previous approval after the key transfer.
- **Documented fix:** Call the _clearApproval function to clear the approval.
- **Report:** https://code4rena.com/reports/2021-11-unlock#h-04-approvals-not-cleared-after-key-transfer
