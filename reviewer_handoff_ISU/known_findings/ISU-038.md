## Published report facts

- **Root cause:** The variable limiting user purchase quantity is not updated after license purchase.
- **Documented consequence:** The attacker calls the claimLicensePartner() or claimLicensePublic() functions multiple times to repeatedly purchase licenses without restriction.
- **Documented fix:** Accurately record each user's purchase quantity to prevent overbuying.
- **Report:** https://github.com/code-423n4/zenith-portfolio/tree/main/reports
