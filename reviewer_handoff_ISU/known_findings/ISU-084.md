## Published report facts

- **Root cause:** Token balances are not promptly updated when the contract switches revenue sources.
- **Documented consequence:** The calculation of balance and shares is incorrect, resulting in users receiving undeserved shares.
- **Documented fix:** Remove calls to the external functions transferFunds and setYieldSource, and restrict swapYieldSource to owner-only access.
- **Report:** https://code4rena.com/reports/2021-07-pooltogether#h-03-setyieldsource-leads-to-temporary-wrong-results
