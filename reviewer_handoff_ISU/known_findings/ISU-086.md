## Published report facts

- **Root cause:** The ids[] array is not reordered to place new loans at the ids[0] position.
- **Documented consequence:** Errors in recording new loans enabled borrowers to create loans that could not be liquidated.
- **Documented fix:** Reorder the ids array to update the new loan entry to ids[0].
- **Report:** https://code4rena.com/reports/2022-11-debtdao#h-05-borrower-can-craft-a-borrow-that-cannot-be-liquidated-even-by-arbiter-
