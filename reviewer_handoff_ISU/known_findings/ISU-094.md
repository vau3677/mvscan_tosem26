## Published report facts

- **Root cause:** The variable for accumulating and storing the remaining debt after each auction is lacking.
- **Documented consequence:** The debt was not properly liquidated, resulting in the auction proceeds going directly to the borrower.
- **Documented fix:** Increase the record of the debt gap through the newly added _shortfall variable.
- **Report:** https://code4rena.com/reports/2022-12-backed#h-01-borrowers-may-earn-auction-proceeds-without-filling-the-debt-shortfall
