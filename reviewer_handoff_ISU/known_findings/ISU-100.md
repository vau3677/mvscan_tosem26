## Published report facts

- **Root cause:** The "amountOutMinimum" parameter is missing for slippage protection.
- **Documented consequence:** The attacker can manipulate the transaction price through a front-running attack, causing the output amount of the swap to be lower than expected.
- **Documented fix:** Pass the correct amountOutMinimum value.
- **Report:** https://code4rena.com/reports/2024-05-bakerfi#h-04-multiple-swap-lack-slippage-protection
