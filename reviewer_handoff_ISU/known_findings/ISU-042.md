## Published report facts

- **Root cause:** expressReceiveTokenWithData() delays state updates until after token transfers.
- **Documented consequence:** Stealing funds in cross-chain transfers using reentry attacks.
- **Documented fix:** Call _setExpressReceiveTokenWithData() in advance.
- **Report:** https://code4rena.com/reports/2023-07-axelar#h-01-expressreceivetoken-can-be-abused-using-reentry
