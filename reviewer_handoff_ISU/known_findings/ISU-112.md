## Published report facts

- **Root cause:** There is a lack of variables for tracking the reward distribution of each point.
- **Documented consequence:** The reward calculation did not correctly reflect the changes in the total points of the pool, affecting the user's rewards.
- **Documented fix:** Introduce the rewardPerTokenStored variable to track the reward distribution for each point.
- **Report:** https://code4rena.com/reports/2023-03-neotokyo#h-01-updating-a-pools-total-points-doesnt-affect-existing-stake-positions-for-rewards-calculation
