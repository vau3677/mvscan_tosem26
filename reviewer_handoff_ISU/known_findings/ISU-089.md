## Published report facts

- **Root cause:** The Lock.extendLock function increases the locked amount but does not update the total locked amount.
- **Documented consequence:** An accounting error resulted in the inability to release locked assets added through the extendLock() function in the future.
- **Documented fix:** Update totalLocked[_asset] in Lock.extendLock.
- **Report:** https://code4rena.com/reports/2022-12-tigris#h-01-locksol-assets-deposited-with-lockextendlock-function-are-lost
