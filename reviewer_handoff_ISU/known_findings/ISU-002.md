## Published report facts

- **Root cause:** The syncVaderPrice function does not update previousPrices after synchronizing prices.
- **Documented consequence:** The attacker uses the lagged previousPrices to calculate the liquidity assessment and manipulate the liquidity share.
- **Documented fix:** Update the previousPrices array after price synchronization.
- **Report:** https://code4rena.com/reports/2021-12-vader#h-10-previousprices-is-never-updated-upon-syncing-token-price
