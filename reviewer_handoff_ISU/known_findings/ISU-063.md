## Published report facts

- **Root cause:** The liquidateClean function does not update the borrower's loan shares.
- **Documented consequence:** The borrower's remaining share is not deducted from its actual debt.
- **Documented fix:** Include the remaining shares in _sharesToLiquidate after liquidation.
- **Report:** https://code4rena.com/reports/2022-08-frax#h-01-any-borrower-with-bad-debt-can-be-liquidated-multiple-times-to-lock-funds-in-the-lending-pair
