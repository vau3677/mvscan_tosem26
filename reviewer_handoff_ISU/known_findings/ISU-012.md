## Published report facts

- **Root cause:** The cleanupLoan() function is called before _updateAndCheckCollateral().
- **Documented consequence:** A reentry attack is utilized to update debt shares multiple times, manipulate borrowing caps, and prevent other users from borrowing.
- **Documented fix:** Call cleanupLoan() after _updateAndCheckCollateral().
- **Report:** https://code4rena.com/reports/2024-03-revert-lend#h-02-risk-of-reentrancy-onerc721received-function-to-manipulate-collateral-token-configs-shares
