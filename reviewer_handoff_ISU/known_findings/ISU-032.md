## Published report facts

- **Root cause:** The liquidation() and rigidRedemption() functions calculate collateralRatio differently.
- **Documented consequence:** Bypass the correct mortgage rate calculation by calculating only the principal amount borrowed and ignoring the share of the borrowing and accumulated fees.
- **Documented fix:** Modify the collateralRatio calculation method in the rigidRedemption() function.
- **Report:** https://code4rena.com/reports/2023-06-lybra#h-02-doesnt-calculate-the-current-borrowing-amount-for-the-provider-including-the-providers-borrowed-shares-and-accumulated-fees-due-to-inconsistency-in-collateralratio-calculation
