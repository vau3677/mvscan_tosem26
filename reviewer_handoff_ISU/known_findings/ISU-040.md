## Published report facts

- **Root cause:** Call previewDeposit() before updateFunding().
- **Documented consequence:** The value of the assets calculated at the time of redemption included additional funds for an undeserved profit.
- **Documented fix:** Move the updateFunding() call to before previewDeposit().
- **Report:** https://code4rena.com/reports/2023-08-dopex#h-05-users-can-get-immediate-profit-when-deposit-and-redeem-in-perpetualatlanticvaultlp
