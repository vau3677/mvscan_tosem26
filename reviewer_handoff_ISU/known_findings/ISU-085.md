## Published report facts

- **Root cause:** The total value locked (TVL) is not promptly updated when users deposit or withdraw.
- **Documented consequence:** Wrong token share calculated using outdated TVL.
- **Documented fix:** First update the TVL, then proceed with other operations when depositing or withdrawing tokens.
- **Report:** https://code4rena.com/reports/2021-12-mellow#h-04-aavevault-does-not-update-tvl-on-depositwithdraw
