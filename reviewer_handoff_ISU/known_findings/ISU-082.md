## Published report facts

- **Root cause:** The index in feederPositionMap is not updated when removing oracle addresses.
- **Documented consequence:** A calculation error caused an out-of-bounds access error when deleting the feeder.
- **Documented fix:** Synchronize updates to oracle indexes moved to new positions in feederPositionMap during deletion.
- **Report:** https://code4rena.com/reports/2022-11-paraspace#h-01-data-corruption-in-nftfloororacle-denial-of-service
