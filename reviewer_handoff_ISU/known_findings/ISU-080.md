## Published report facts

- **Root cause:** The auctionBurn function does not update ibRatio when burning auction bonds.
- **Documented consequence:** The calculation of total supply was incorrect, resulting in some assets being frozen.
- **Documented fix:** Update ibRatio based on the withdrawn amount in the auctionBurn function.
- **Report:** https://code4rena.com/reports/2021-09-defiprotocol#h-02-basketsolauctionburn-a-failed-auction-will-freeze-part-of-the-funds
