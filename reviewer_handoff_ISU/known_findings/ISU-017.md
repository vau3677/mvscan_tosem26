## Published report facts

- **Root cause:** Calculate the allocation ratio for each faction's NFTs based on the current totalVotingPower.
- **Documented consequence:** The users’ allocation shares were miscalculated, allowing the attacker to obtain more funds than they deserved by destroying NFTs.
- **Documented fix:** Do not use dynamic totalVotingPower for allocation. Instead, record the initial totalVotingPower at the time of each distribution.
- **Report:** https://code4rena.com/reports/2023-05-party#h-01-the-distribution-logic-will-be-broken-after-calling-ragequit
