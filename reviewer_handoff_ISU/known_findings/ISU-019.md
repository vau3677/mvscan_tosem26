## Published report facts

- **Root cause:** A type field is missing in the signature to distinguish different operation types.
- **Documented consequence:** The attacker used the signature of refinanceFull() to prepend addNewTranche(), causing the loan amount to be approximately twice the original amount.
- **Documented fix:** Add a type field in the RenegotiationOffer to distinguish different operation types.
- **Report:** https://code4rena.com/reports/2024-04-gondi#h-17-refinancefulladdnewtranche-reusing-a-lenders-signature-leads-to-unintended-behavior
