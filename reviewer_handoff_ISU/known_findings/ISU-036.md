## Published report facts

- **Root cause:** The variable idleETH is not synchronized.
- **Documented consequence:** An error in the calculation of idleETH caused ETH withdrawn from the vault to be permanently locked in the Giant Pool.
- **Documented fix:** Add an update for idleETH.
- **Report:** https://code4rena.com/reports/2022-11-stakehouse#h-06-bringunusedethbackintogiantpool-can-cause-stuck-ether-funds-in-giant-pool
