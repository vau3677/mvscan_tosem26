## Published report facts

- **Root cause:** The profit distribution ratio is calculated before updating the dependent values.
- **Documented consequence:** Using inaccurate values ​​when allocating profits leads to incorrect profit distribution, causing LPs (liquidity providers) to receive too much profit.
- **Documented fix:** Call syncGlobalCollateral() before calling handleProfit().
- **Report:** https://code4rena.com/reports/2023-02-malt#h-05-_distributeprofit-will-use-the-stale-globalicswingtradercollateraldeficitswingtradercollateralratio-which-will-result-in-incorrect-profit-distribution
