## Published report facts

- **Root cause:** The state variable totalLpETH is not locked to equal totalSupply.
- **Documented consequence:** Pre- and post-operations (sandwich attack) to donate ETH and directly obtain lpETH.
- **Documented fix:** Limit the claimedAmount calculation to only the ETH amount obtained from LRT swaps.
- **Report:** https://code4rena.com/reports/2024-05-loop#h-01-availability-of-deposit-invariant-can-be-bypassed
