## Published report facts

- **Root cause:** userTotalStaked is not updated when releasing funds.
- **Documented consequence:** The actual pledge amount does not match userTotalStaked, thus miscalculating the withdrawn funds at the time of withdrawal.
- **Documented fix:** Update the userTotalStaked in release().
- **Report:** https://code4rena.com/reports/2024-03-gitcoin#h-01-usertotalstaked-invariant-will-be-broken-due-to-vulnerable-implementations-in-release
