## Published report facts

- **Root cause:** The _shareMochi() function incorrectly resets treasuryShare.
- **Documented consequence:** VeCRV holders can receive tokens that should have been allocated to treasure multiple times.
- **Documented fix:** Remove the operation that resets treasuryShare.
- **Report:** https://code4rena.com/reports/2021-10-mochi#h-11-treasuryshare-is-overwritten-in-feepoolv0_sharemochi
