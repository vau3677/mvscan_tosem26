## Published report facts

- **Root cause:** The fee withdrawal status is not updated.
- **Documented consequence:** The withdrawFee() function does not have any protection mechanism, allowing the protocol fee to be withdrawn multiple times.
- **Documented fix:** Track the protocol fee withdrawal status.
- **Report:** https://code4rena.com/reports/2023-01-rabbithole#h-02-protocol-fees-can-be-withdrawn-multiple-times-in-erc20quest
