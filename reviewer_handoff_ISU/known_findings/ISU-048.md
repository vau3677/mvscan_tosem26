## Published report facts

- **Root cause:** toiletState[tokenId].plotId is not updated when users transfer tokens to another plot.
- **Documented consequence:** Allowing multiple tenants to occupy the same plot and causing errors in reward calculations.
- **Documented fix:** Update toiletState[tokenId].plotId in the transferToUnoccupiedPlot() function.
- **Report:** https://code4rena.com/reports/2024-07-munchables#h-01-single-plot-can-be-occupied-by-multiple-renters
