## Published report facts

- **Root cause:** After the purchase order is matched, when the ERC quantity is lower than the defined minimum value, the order will not be added to the market for initialization.
- **Documented consequence:** A partially filled short record has no associated short order, which makes it impossible for the user to exit the short position or perform liquidation operations.
- **Documented fix:** After the call of matchIncomingSell(), make the Short Record reach the fully filled (SR.FullyFilled) state.
- **Report:** https://code4rena.com/reports/2024-03-dittoeth#h-04-partially-filled-short-records-created-without-a-short-order-cannot-be-liquidated-and-exited
