## Published report facts

- **Root cause:** The previous getApproved[_tokenId] approval status is not revoked.
- **Documented consequence:** Approval was not revoked after the NFT was transferred, and the previous owner transferred the NFT back to himself through repeated transactions.
- **Documented fix:** Clear the approval status after transferring the NFT.
- **Report:** https://code4rena.com/reports/2024-08-superposition#h-02-unrevoked-approvals-allow-nft-recovery-by-previous-owner
