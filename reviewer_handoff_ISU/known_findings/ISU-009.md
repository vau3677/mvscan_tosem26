## Published report facts

- **Root cause:** The syncRewards() function is not called to synchronize rewards before executing withdrawal operations.
- **Documented consequence:** An accounting logic error caused the nextRewards calculation to be inflated, resulting in overpayment of rewards.
- **Documented fix:** Call syncRewards() before calling beforeWithdraw().
- **Report:** https://code4rena.com/reports/2022-09-frax#h-01-wrong-accounting-logic-when-syncrewards-is-called-within-beforewithdraw-makes-withdrawals-impossible
