# Start here

This folder contains one independent review of 116 published inconsistent-state findings.

Use these files:

- `ISU_GUIDE.md` — short instructions and the `Y`/`N`/`U` definitions
- `known_findings/README.md` — the 116 readable finding cards, in order
- `isu_reviewer_1.csv` — the sheet to complete

For each row:

1. Open the card named in the `known_finding` column.
2. Read the published root cause, consequence, and fix.
3. Enter `Y`, `N`, or `U` in `is_mvsi`.
4. Add a short `correction_note` only when the answer is `N` or `U`.

Do not inspect MV-Scan output or another reviewer's answers. The research team will compare the independent reviews, adjudicate disagreements, and perform detector matching afterward.
