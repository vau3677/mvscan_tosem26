# ISU-087: 2024-08-phi#h-04

## Published report facts

- **Root cause:** The endTime is incorrectly updated to block.timestamp in updateArtSettings().
- **Documented consequence:** The attacker exploited the extended endTime to repeat the transaction and mint additional tokens.
- **Documented fix:** Separate the update logic for "uri", "soulBounded", and "royalty" config. Additionally, change the ">" to ">=" in the time validation condition.
- **Report:** https://code4rena.com/reports/2024-08-phi#h-04-forced-endtime-extension-in-updateartsettings-allows-attacker-to-mint-more-tokens
