# ISU-025: 2023-02-malt#h-03

## Published report facts

- **Root cause:** Claim rewards when the TWAP falls below the protocol's price threshold.
- **Documented consequence:** The attacker received two defaultIncentive rewards through flash loans in two consecutive blocks.
- **Documented fix:** Adjust the reward distribution conditions by adding a cooldown period.
- **Report:** https://code4rena.com/reports/2023-02-malt#h-03-manipulation-of-liveprice-to-receive-defaultincentive-in-2-consecutive-blocks
