# ISU-105: 2024-01-salty#h-02

## Published report facts

- **Root cause:** The virtualRewards is not initialized when liquidity is first provided to the protocol.
- **Documented consequence:** Ability to incorrectly claim all rewards in the pool due to lack of virtual reward deductions.
- **Documented fix:** Call performUpkeep() before the initial distribution of the contract to achieve initialization.
- **Report:** https://code4rena.com/reports/2024-01-salty#h-02-first-liquidity-provider-can-claim-all-initial-pool-rewards
