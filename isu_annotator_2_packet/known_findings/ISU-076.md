# ISU-076: 2024-04-gondi#h-07

## Published report facts

- **Root cause:** The data in _queueAccounting[lastQueueIndex] is not cleared when deploying a new withdrawal queue.
- **Documented consequence:** queueClaimAll() used outdated data and incorrectly allocated the total amount due.
- **Documented fix:** Clear _queueAccounting[lastQueueIndex] data in deployWithdrawalQueue().
- **Report:** https://code4rena.com/reports/2024-04-gondi#h-07-deploywithdrawalqueue-need-to-clear-_queueaccountinglastqueueindex
