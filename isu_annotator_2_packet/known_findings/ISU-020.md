# ISU-020: 2024-06-playfi-proleague#h-2

## Published report facts

- **Root cause:** Send the referral commission before updating state variables (totalClaimed, totalLicenses).
- **Documented consequence:** The attacker used a reentrancy attack to purchase quantities exceeding the permitted limit.
- **Documented fix:** Process referral commission transfers after updating state variables (totalClaimed, totalLicenses).
- **Report:** https://github.com/code-423n4/zenith-portfolio
