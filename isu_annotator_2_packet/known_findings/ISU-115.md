# ISU-115: 2024-08-superposition#h-01

## Published report facts

- **Root cause:** The update_emergency_council_7_D_0_C_1_C_58() function fails to correctly update the target state (i.e., the emergency_council address) and instead erroneously modifies another administrative role, nft_manager.
- **Documented consequence:** An incorrect update to nft_manager caused emergency management to not work properly.
- **Documented fix:** Update the emergency_council address.
- **Report:** https://code4rena.com/reports/2024-08-superposition#h-01-update_emergency_council_7_d_0_c_1_c_58-updates-nft-manager-instead-of-emergency-council
