# ISU-097: 2021-12-amun#h-01

## Published report facts

- **Root cause:** The remaining tokens are not refunded.
- **Documented consequence:** Incorrect calculation and refund of remaining underlying tokens.
- **Documented fix:** Calculate and trade only the required token amount, then refund any remaining tokens to the user after the transaction.
- **Report:** https://code4rena.com/reports/2021-12-amun#h-01-unused-erc20-tokens-are-not-refunded-and-can-be-stolen-by-attacker
