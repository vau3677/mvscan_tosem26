# ISU-031: 2023-02-malt#h-02

## Published report facts

- **Root cause:** The location of the _fillInEpochGaps() call is incorrect.
- **Documented consequence:** fillInEpochGaps() did not correctly calculate and request rewards from overflowPool, resulting in lost rewards for unprofitable epochs.
- **Documented fix:** Remove the call to fillInEpochGaps(). Or, move the call to checkRewardUnderflow() to the beginning of each new epoch. Or, restrict calls to fillInEpochGaps() to administrators only.
- **Report:** https://code4rena.com/reports/2023-02-malt#h-02-rewardthrottle-if-an-epoch-does-not-have-any-profit-then-there-may-not-be-rewards-for-that-epoch-at-the-start-of-the-next-epoch
