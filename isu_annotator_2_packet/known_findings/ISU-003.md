# ISU-003: 2023-02-malt#h-06

## Published report facts

- **Root cause:** Call syncGlobalCollateral() at the end of stabilize().
- **Documented consequence:** Using an outdated collateralRatio for stabilization calculations can make stabilization operations inaccurate and may cause tokens to depeg.
- **Documented fix:** Call impliedCollateralService() before stabilize() executes.
- **Report:** https://code4rena.com/reports/2023-02-malt#h-06-stabilizernodestabilize-uses-stale-globalimpliedcollateralservice-data-which-will-make-stabilize-incorrect
