# ISU-102: 2023-01-astaria#h-17

## Published report facts

- **Root cause:** The latest interest is not counted into the total assets.
- **Documented consequence:** The next cycle cannot be started correctly and users' funds cannot be withdrawn.
- **Documented fix:** Call accrue() at the beginning of processEpoch() to account for the latest interest.
- **Report:** https://code4rena.com/reports/2023-01-astaria#h-17-function-processepoch-in-publicvault-would-revert-when-most-of-the-users-withdraw-their-funds-because-of-the-underflow-for-new-yintercept-calculation
