# ISU-069: 2023-01-ondo#h-01

## Published report facts

- **Root cause:** The variable totalBurned is not updated.
- **Documented consequence:** The amount received by the user during subsequent redemption is inaccurate, resulting in financial loss.
- **Documented fix:** After refunding refundedAmt, update totalBurned to deduct the refunded amount.
- **Report:** https://code4rena.com/reports/2023-01-ondo#h-01-loss-of-user-funds-when-completing-cash-redemptions
