# ISU-001: 2023-01-popcorn#h-11

## Published report facts

- **Root cause:** The highWaterMark is updated whenever a user deposits, withdraws, or mints.
- **Documented consequence:** The inability to properly track the progress of earnings makes it impossible for the agreement to correctly charge for earnings when they are available.
- **Documented fix:** Adjust the highWaterMark update timing to occur only during initial deposits and after successful fee collections.
- **Report:** https://code4rena.com/reports/2023-01-popcorn#h-11-protocol-loses-fees-because-highwatermark-is-updated-every-time-someone-deposit-withdraw-mint
