# ISU-013: 2024-01-curves#h-02

## Published report facts

- **Root cause:** Update accumulated rewards only during trades, not transfers.
- **Documented consequence:** The fee offset for the new account was not updated, resulting in incorrect calculation of the fees due to the account.
- **Documented fix:** Call onBalanceChange() to update accumulated rewards in all balance-changing operations.
- **Report:** https://code4rena.com/reports/2024-01-curves#h-02-unrestricted-claiming-of-fees-due-to-missing-balance-updates-in-feesplitter
