# ISU-039: 2023-01-astaria#h-06

## Published report facts

- **Root cause:** The variable for interest calculation remains unupdated during buyout operations.
- **Documented consequence:** The slope of the new lien tokens is not added to the total slope of the public vault, borrowers are not paid interest, and liquidity providers cannot receive rewards.
- **Documented fix:** Add the new lien slope back to the total slope after buyout completion.
- **Report:** https://code4rena.com/reports/2023-01-astaria#h-06-buying-out-corrupts-the-slope-of-a-vault-reducing-rewards-of-lps
