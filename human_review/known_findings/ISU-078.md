# ISU-078: 2024-03-dittoeth#h-07

## Published report facts

- **Root cause:** updatedAt is not updated when creating proposals.
- **Documented consequence:** The collateral ratio was calculated incorrectly and was lower than that recorded in other valid redemption proposals, thus successfully initiating an invalid dispute and obtaining penalty fees.
- **Documented fix:** Update updatedAt in the decreaseCollateral() function.
- **Report:** https://code4rena.com/reports/2024-03-dittoeth#h-07-valid-redemption-proposals-can-be-disputed-by-decreasing-collateral
