# ISU-114: 2021-05-visorfinance#h-02

## Published report facts

- **Root cause:** The granted NFT transaction authorization is not revoked.
- **Documented consequence:** The authorization has not been revoked, and the authorized person can transfer NFTs repeatedly.
- **Documented fix:** Modify the status variables that control the granting or revocation of permissions.
- **Report:** https://code4rena.com/reports/2021-05-visorfinance#h-02-nft-transfer-approvals-are-not-removed-and-cannot-be-revoked-thus-leading-to-loss-of-nft-tokens
