# ISU-110: 2021-09-sushitrident-2#h-03

## Published report facts

- **Root cause:** The reclaimIncentive function does not decrement the incentive.rewardsUnclaimed field.
- **Documented consequence:** The attacker repeatedly called the recaaimIncentive function to steal all tokens in the incentive pool.
- **Documented fix:** Decrement the incentive.rewardsUnclaimed field by the withdrawn amount.
- **Report:** https://code4rena.com/reports/2021-09-sushitrident-2#h-03-concentratedliquiditypoolmanagers-incentives-can-be-stolen
