# ISU-058: 2021-05-visorfinance#h-03

## Published report facts

- **Root cause:** Failure to clear the approval authorization for NFT resulted in authorized agents being able to transfer the NFT multiple times.
- **Documented consequence:** The NFT authorization is not reset.
- **Documented fix:** Reset the approval on transfer.
- **Report:** https://code4rena.com/reports/2021-05-visorfinance#h-03-approval-for-nft-transfers-is-not-removed-after-transfer
