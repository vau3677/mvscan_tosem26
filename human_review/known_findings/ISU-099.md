# ISU-099: 2021-10-mochi#h-06

## Published report facts

- **Root cause:** The claimRewardAsMochi function does not reduce the user's reward balance.
- **Documented consequence:** The referrer can repeatedly claim the same reward until the fee pool is exhausted.
- **Documented fix:** Decrement the user's reward balance in the claimRewardAsMochi function.
- **Report:** https://code4rena.com/reports/2021-10-mochi#h-06-referrer-can-drain-referralfeepoolv0
