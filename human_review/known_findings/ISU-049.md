# ISU-049: 2023-05-particle#h-06

## Published report facts

- **Root cause:** There is a lack of a flag for indicating that the purchase operation of the contract is underway.
- **Documented consequence:** onERC721Received() creates two pledge rights in the intermediate state, resulting in the same NFT being bound to two different pledge rights.
- **Documented fix:** Set a _marketBuyFlow flag to indicate that the contract is conducting a market purchase operation.
