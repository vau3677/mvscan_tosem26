# ISU-066: 2021-07-wildcredit#h-02

## Published report facts

- **Root cause:** The accumulated interest rate is not updated during account liquidation.
- **Documented consequence:** Interest calculation error, the borrower bypassed interest payment through self liquidation.
- **Documented fix:** Revise the previous accumulated interest rate update method.
- **Report:** https://code4rena.com/reports/2021-07-wildcredit#h-02-lendingpairliquidateaccount-does-not-accrue-and-update-cumulativeinterestrate
