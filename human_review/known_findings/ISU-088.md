# ISU-088: 2022-09-party#h-05

## Published report facts

- **Root cause:** The NFT approval is not revoked.
- **Documented consequence:** The attacker cancels the proposal in progress, thereby bypassing the protection measures and stealing the NFT.
- **Documented fix:** Clear the authorization after the proposal is canceled.
