# ISU-022: 2022-11-stakehouse#h-16

## Published report facts

- **Root cause:** beforeTokenTransfer() is called before the ETH transfer occurs.
- **Documented consequence:** After receiving ETH, the attacker used the unlocked claimRewards() function to perform multiple transactions, resulting in too many rewards being issued.
- **Documented fix:** Perform the "idleETH -= _amount;" operation after burning the LP tokens.
- **Report:** https://code4rena.com/reports/2022-11-stakehouse#h-16-reentrancy-vulnerability-in-giantmevandfeespoolwithdraweth
